Before You Schedule

Pricing, access, timing, property type, and specialty services can change the scope of the cleaning plan.

How does pricing work?

Pricing depends on the property, service type, cleaning scope, frequency, building size, and schedule. Contact Paradigm Janitorial Services for a custom estimate.

Do you take special requests?

Yes. Special services can be scheduled when the team and equipment needed for the request are available. Advance notice is helpful.

What safety measures do you take?

Paradigm uses proper cleaning signage and professional cleaning practices. The current service information also states that the cleaning team is insured and bonded.

What building sizes do you clean for window service?

Window cleaning is available for businesses, residential homes, and apartment buildings, including accessible multi story windows up to four stories.

What types of properties do you clean?

Paradigm serves residential homes, duplexes, apartment buildings, offices, retail spaces, learning facilities, warehouses, industrial properties, and construction projects.

Can cleaning happen outside normal business hours?

Yes. Cleaning schedules can be planned around operating hours, evenings, mornings, weekends, and other access windows.

Do you offer day porter service?

Day porter service can be included for appropriate commercial properties that need support while the building is active.

Do you provide power washing and window cleaning for homes and businesses?

Yes. Power washing and window cleaning are available for residential and commercial properties based on site access and service requirements.
