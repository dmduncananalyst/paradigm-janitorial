Completed in the website code

All 33 pages have a unique title, meta description, canonical URL, one H1,
indexing and preview directives, Open Graph metadata and X/Twitter card metadata.
Social preview images have absolute URLs, dimensions and alternative text.

All 33 pages are reachable from the home page through ordinary HTML links.
The XML sitemap contains exactly those 33 canonical pages and their primary
images. robots.txt allows crawling and points to that sitemap. Existing page
filenames were preserved so these edits do not break existing inbound links.

There are 32 visible breadcrumb trails with matching BreadcrumbList markup.
The JSON-LD graph connects the business, website, page and primary image.
The site includes 17 Service entities, 11 Articles with matching direct answers,
the FAQ page with its 8 visible questions and answers, the Clean Spaces article
collection, and appropriate AboutPage and ContactPage types.

Eighteen home/service/location introductions now state the service, scope and
service area directly. Existing article answers and related service links were
preserved. Important answers are ordinary HTML text and do not require a chatbot.

email, counties and contact hours. A verified public street address was not
supplied. No address, map coordinates, ratings, reviews, affiliations, social
profiles or publishing dates were invented. LocalBusiness rich-result markup
can be added when a verified public business address is supplied.

SEO-AEO-AUDIT.csv lists each page's title, description, canonical URL, social
