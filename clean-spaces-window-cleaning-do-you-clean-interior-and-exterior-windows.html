Do you clean interior and exterior windows?

Interior and exterior window cleaning can be arranged, but both sides should be explicitly included in the service agreement. Reachability, building access, glass condition and equipment requirements determine which panes can be serviced safely.

Identify both sides of the requested glass

A proposal should distinguish interior, exterior and any inaccessible panes. Glass partitions inside an office are different from exterior facade windows, even if both are called windows. Include door glass and sidelights only when intended. Listing the areas prevents one side from being assumed complete simply because the opposite side was cleaned.

Account for different access conditions

Interior access can be limited by furniture, tenant schedules or restricted rooms. Exterior access can be limited by landscaping, height or physical obstructions. A provider should assess these conditions before committing to the work. Do not assume a pane reachable from indoors can be safely cleaned on its outside face by leaning through an opening.

Recognize conditions cleaning cannot fix

Condensation or cloudiness between sealed panes cannot usually be reached through cleaning either exposed side. Damaged seals, scratched glass and finish defects may require another trade. Discuss those conditions separately from removable fingerprints and surface soil. Clear expectations help the property evaluate the finished service without expecting glass repairs from a cleaning appointment.

Avoid assumptions about inaccessible panes

A window can have reachable interior glass and an exterior side requiring another access arrangement. A provider should identify this before claiming to cover both sides of every pane. Ask whether the proposal lists excluded or inaccessible areas and who will report them.

For shared office buildings, interior access may also depend on individual tenants. Building permission alone may not authorize entry into an occupied suite. Make the access responsibility clear so an incomplete side is not mistaken for a failure to perform a task that was actually unavailable.

Apply the decision to the whole window area

Consider the glass together with the area around it. A door may have a film, a display may prevent interior access and a sill may hold personal belongings. Exterior panes can also have a different access requirement from the room inside. Tell the provider about these conditions so the proposed service is realistic. The property should know which components are included and which remain outside the scope. If a problem concerns a damaged seal, coating or piece of hardware, arrange an appropriate assessment rather than assume another glass wash will solve it. A clear result means understanding both what improved and what cleaning could not address.

A practical example

An office may request interior lobby partitions and exterior entrance glass while excluding upper facade windows. That is a legitimate limited scope when it is stated clearly, rather than described as every window in the building.
