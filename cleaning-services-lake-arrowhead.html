Paradigm Janitorial provides commercial and residential cleaning in Lake Arrowhead. Cleaning for Lake Arrowhead second homes and managed properties

Lake Arrowhead has a substantial second home market, with properties used during weekends, holidays, and summer stays. A residence can need preparation before occupants return, care for agreed unused areas, or cleaning after completed work. Property cleaning can include floors, windows, and specified interior and exterior spaces while local business premises can request commercial service.

A home preparing for occupants may need different work from a regularly used residence. The property contact can identify which rooms and exterior areas are available, whether contractors have completed their work, and what surface care is requested. The quote can describe those needs without treating every mountain home as a rental.

Commercial cleaning alongside residential care

Local business premises can also request office, retail, restaurant, and ordinary janitorial cleaning. Property boundaries, shared spaces, and accessible windows should be identified separately. For residential work, estate care, floor maintenance, and project preparation can be combined when appropriate and confirmed in the agreed scope.

Recurring cleaning, occupied hours support, and preparation after renovation are different requests. Tell Paradigm which applies to your property so the quote can identify the work and visit arrangements. Services may be combined, but specialty work is confirmed in the scope rather than assumed to be included.
