Should You Offer Your Employees a 401(k)?

Consider the potential advantages and responsibilities of an employee retirement plan.

A retirement benefit employees may appreciate

Offering a 401(k) can give eligible employees a convenient way to save from their paychecks. For some workplaces, a retirement plan is an appealing part of the overall benefits package. It is not the only way to support employees, and it may not be right for every business.

What employers should consider

Plans can involve administrative costs, recordkeeping, fiduciary responsibilities, eligibility rules, and required notices. An employer match is optional in some plan designs but required in others. Comparing providers and plan structures is important.

Potential tax considerations

Eligible employer contributions may be deductible, and certain small employers may qualify for tax credits when starting an eligible retirement plan. The requirements vary, so businesses should confirm eligibility with a qualified adviser.

A reasonable place to start

Ask employees whether retirement benefits would be useful, estimate a realistic budget, and compare a traditional 401(k), safe harbor 401(k), SIMPLE IRA, or other available arrangements. Consider whether the business can administer the chosen plan consistently.

A workplace also needs everyday care

Benefits are one part of the employee experience; the condition of the workplace matters, too. Office managers can keep reception areas, staff kitchens, meeting rooms and restrooms on a clear maintenance schedule alongside their other operating priorities.
