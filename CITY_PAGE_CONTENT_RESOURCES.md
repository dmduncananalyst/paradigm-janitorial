# City-page content refresh — October 8, 2026

Updated 21 city janitorial service pages to reduce repeated copy and make the content more useful to property managers. Each page has distinct descriptions of relevant property types, service-scope examples, a three-point quote-preparation checklist, two practical FAQs and three independently maintained public resources. Resources are labeled and link to official city/state/federal agencies. The existing hero photos, calls to action, service links, county navigation and URLs remain unchanged.

Important: Descriptions are service-planning examples, not claims of prior work, customer reviews, specific pricing, city offices or confirmed coverage. Paradigm should verify operational coverage and availability before indexing. Public-agency links are informational and are not endorsements. Link relevance and accessibility should be reviewed periodically because external sites change.
