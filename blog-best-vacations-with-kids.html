Best Family Vacations With Kids

Family vacations are easier to plan when you start with what your kids actually enjoy, then choose a destination that fits the pace of your family.

The best family vacation is not always the place with the longest list of attractions. It is the place that fits your children, your energy level, and the kind of memories you actually want to make together. Some families want beaches and easy afternoons, some want rides and nonstop activity, and others want wildlife, museums, or a first big adventure outdoors.

San Diego, California

San Diego is a strong choice when you want variety without making every day feel complicated. Families can spend one day at the beach, another at the San Diego Zoo or Safari Park, and another exploring museums, parks, or the waterfront. The official San Diego tourism site highlights 70 miles of coastline along with major family attractions including the zoo, Safari Park, SeaWorld, and LEGOLAND California.

Why kids may love it: Animals, beaches, tide pools, rides, and plenty of outdoor space give children different kinds of fun during the same trip.

Why parents may love it: You can mix bigger attraction days with slower beach or park days, which can make a family vacation feel less exhausting.

Orlando, Florida

For kids who love characters, rides, water parks, and big attractions, Orlando is hard to ignore. Visit Orlando describes the city as a destination designed for kids of different ages, with theme parks as the obvious draw and additional nature, outdoor, dining, and family activities beyond the parks.

Why kids may love it: There is a lot of excitement in one destination, especially for children who have been dreaming about a favorite park or attraction.

Why parents should plan ahead: Big park days can be long. Build in rest, choose the attractions that matter most to your family, and avoid trying to do everything in one trip.

Yellowstone National Park

Yellowstone gives children a completely different kind of vacation. Geysers, wildlife, lakes, forests, and the Junior Ranger program can turn the trip into an outdoor adventure that also feels educational. The National Park Service encourages families to plan ahead because roads, weather, lodging, and services can change with the season.

Why kids may love it: Seeing a geyser erupt or spotting wildlife in the real world can feel very different from anything they experience at home.

Why parents should plan ahead: Yellowstone is enormous, cell service can be limited, and wildlife safety matters. Decide which areas you most want to see before arriving and leave room in the schedule for travel time inside the park.

Washington, DC

Washington, DC works especially well for curious kids who enjoy museums, history, science, and interactive exhibits. The city has major family attractions, monuments, museums, and a number of free activities, which can help balance the cost of a family trip.

Why kids may love it: The trip can include everything from hands on museum exhibits to monuments, animals, history, and outdoor exploring.

Why parents may love it: A vacation can be entertaining and educational at the same time, and many of the best known experiences do not require an admission fee.

Choose the trip for your kids, not somebody else’s kids

A toddler who loves sand and animals may have a very different idea of a perfect vacation than a teenager who wants rides, city exploring, or outdoor adventure. Think about how much walking your children can comfortably handle, whether they enjoy busy schedules, how they do with long travel days, and whether your family needs downtime between activities.

The best trip is the one where everyone gets something they are excited about. You do not need to pack every hour. Leave space for a slow breakfast, an unexpected stop, a pool afternoon, or the moment your kids decide that the simple thing you almost skipped is their favorite part of the entire vacation.
