Where to Fish in Los Angeles County, Lakes and Ocean Fishing

Freshwater and saltwater fishing options for Los Angeles County anglers.

Freshwater fishing

Castaic Lake is a major freshwater destination in northern Los Angeles County. Other options to investigate include Puddingstone Reservoir at Frank G. Bonelli Regional Park, Santa Fe Dam Recreation Area, and Legg Lake at Whittier Narrows. Rules, access, stocking, and permitted fishing areas differ.

Fishing from the coast

Anglers can explore public piers and shoreline fishing opportunities in the Los Angeles County coastal area, including Santa Monica Pier, Redondo Beach Pier, and the shoreline around Marina del Rey where fishing is permitted. Always check local access restrictions and safety notices.

Going out on a boat

For ocean fishing beyond shore access, look for licensed passenger fishing boats and charter operators departing from Marina del Rey, Redondo Beach, and the Long Beach or San Pedro area. Compare trip duration, equipment policies, species targets, and cancellation terms.

Before you go

California fishing license requirements, pier exceptions, species limits, seasons, marine protected areas, and local restrictions can change. Verify current rules with the California Department of Fish and Wildlife before fishing.

Clean entrances after busy outdoor weekends

After outdoor outings, tracked-in dirt and grit can build up in building entrances, stairways and shared floors. For businesses and managed properties in Los Angeles County, an agreed floor-care schedule helps keep those high-traffic areas presentable.
