Do I need to be present during window cleaning?

You do not always need to be present during window cleaning if the provider has authorized access and clear instructions. Interior work, locked areas or a required inspection may need an on site contact, so confirm the arrangement before the appointment.

Separate exterior and interior access

Exterior glass may be reachable without entering the premises, but that does not mean the entire job can proceed unattended. Gates, courtyards and secured areas may still require access. Interior glass generally requires permission to enter the rooms involved. Tell the provider which doors will be available and whether a tenant or building manager must authorize entry.

Prepare the areas included in the scope

Furniture, displays and sensitive items near windows can affect access. Decide who moves them and identify anything the cleaning crew must leave untouched. Do not assume workers can relocate valuable merchandise or confidential documents. If an area cannot be reached as agreed, the provider should report the limitation rather than improvise around property restrictions.

Plan an appropriate handoff

An authorized contact can be reachable without remaining beside the crew throughout the visit. Agree on how completion and any limitations will be communicated. If your property requires a walkthrough, make sure the responsible person can attend at the agreed time. Avoid sharing access codes casually just to make an unattended appointment possible.

Make unattended access accountable

The property should identify who is authorized to let workers in and which rooms may be entered. A neighboring tenant or an unlocked door is not automatically permission. Confirm the arrangement in advance and ensure the provider can contact someone if the expected access is unavailable.

For interior work, decide what happens to blinds, displays and nearby belongings. The crew should not have to infer permission to move valuables. A completion message can report a blocked area so management understands the result without requiring someone to watch the entire visit.

Apply the decision to the whole window area

Consider the glass together with the area around it. A door may have a film, a display may prevent interior access and a sill may hold personal belongings. Exterior panes can also have a different access requirement from the room inside. Tell the provider about these conditions so the proposed service is realistic. The property should know which components are included and which remain outside the scope. If a problem concerns a damaged seal, coating or piece of hardware, arrange an appropriate assessment rather than assume another glass wash will solve it. A clear result means understanding both what improved and what cleaning could not address.

A practical example

A retail manager may authorize exterior storefront work before opening but arrange interior glass after staff arrive. A different property may permit both through a building manager. The right arrangement depends on access authorization, not a universal attendance rule.
