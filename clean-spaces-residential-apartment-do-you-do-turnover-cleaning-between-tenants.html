Do you do turnover cleaning between tenants?

Apartment turnover cleaning can prepare the agreed interior areas after a tenant leaves and before the next occupancy. The provider should confirm the tasks, property condition and access window, because cleaning does not automatically include repairs, removal of abandoned belongings or specialist treatment.

Place cleaning at the right point in the turnover

Maintenance and repair work can create fresh dust and debris. Coordinate the cleaning appointment with the property team so the space is ready for the intended service. Identify which fixtures and appliances are installed and available. A turnover program works better when the cleaning crew is not arriving before other necessary work has been completed.

List the unit components involved

Bathrooms, kitchen surfaces, accessible cabinets and floors may have different condition and material requirements. Specify interiors and exteriors where relevant rather than assuming every component is included. Appliance handling and window work should also be clarified. The written scope should give management a useful basis for reviewing the unit before the next tenant arrives.

Separate cleaning from unresolved property problems

A persistent odor, damaged surface or suspected moisture problem may need assessment beyond ordinary cleaning. Report those conditions and clarify who is responsible for addressing them. A clean appearance is not proof that the apartment has no repair needs. The final handoff should distinguish completed cleaning from remaining property issues.

Review the unit against its agreed turnover scope

An empty cupboard interior, an appliance exterior and a window track are separate components. Confirm which are included so management can review the result fairly. A general move ready description should not substitute for a clear list of the spaces and tasks involved.

If a concern remains after cleaning, identify whether it involves removable soil, damage or an ongoing source of odor or moisture. Additional cleaning does not repair every defect. The property should know which issues require maintenance before arranging the next tenancy rather than treating a completed cleaning visit as proof that all preparation is finished.

Consider residents and shared spaces together

Apartment cleaning takes place in a community where residents continue to use entrances, corridors and shared facilities. Explain access rules and which belongings must remain untouched. A cleaning visit should not become an assumption that personal property can be discarded or occupied units can be entered without authorization. Management remains responsible for property rules and maintenance decisions outside the cleaning agreement. When a shared space changes or a new amenity opens, review the materials and duties before adding it to the program. This keeps service useful to the community while making the boundary between cleaning, management and repairs understandable to everyone involved.

A practical example

If painters return after a unit has been cleaned, another visit may be needed for newly created soil. Coordinating the turnover sequence at a property management level avoids confusing new work residue with an incomplete original cleaning.
