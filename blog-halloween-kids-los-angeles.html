Things to Do in Los Angeles With the Kids for Halloween

Ideas for family friendly Halloween outings and ways to check event details.

Pumpkin patches and fall festivals

Pumpkin patches can be a good choice for younger children who enjoy daytime activities, decorations, and photos. Check whether a venue offers games, rides, or admission packages before leaving home.

Neighborhood trick or treating

Choose a familiar, walkable neighborhood, travel together, and plan for traffic and visibility after dark. Families can check local community calendars for organized trick or treat events.

Museums, gardens, and community programs

Libraries, museums, recreation centers, and parks sometimes offer seasonal workshops or family activities. Look for age recommendations and ticket policies.

Planning the outing

Confirm the official 2026 date, opening hours, age suitability, parking, accessibility, and any costume rules directly with each organizer. Events may change or sell out.

Keep shared entrances ready for Halloween traffic

Seasonal decorations and visitors can mean more debris at storefront doors, lobby floors and residential walkways. For apartment communities and other managed properties, a simple before-and-after cleaning schedule can help shared spaces recover from a busy holiday.
