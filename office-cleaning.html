Office Cleaning Throughout the Workday

Office cleaning can include reception areas, workspaces, meeting rooms, restrooms, breakrooms, floors, and glass. Paradigm Janitorial Services schedules work around employees, visitors, and access in Los Angeles County and San Bernardino County.

Cleaning Options for Offices

Recurring janitorial service and porter support can be combined based on traffic, operating hours, and the areas that need repeat attention.

Daily or Recurring Janitorial Service

Vacuuming, dusting, trash removal, restroom care, breakroom cleaning, surface cleaning, and floor maintenance can be scheduled around your operating hours.

Day/Night Porter Service

For offices with steady employee or client traffic, daytime support can help keep restrooms, kitchens, lobbies, conference rooms, and high traffic areas under control.

Client Facing Spaces

Custom Cleaning Scope

Every office uses space differently. Service can be adjusted around staff size, visitor traffic, floor types, breakrooms, restrooms, and sensitive work areas.

What to share when requesting office cleaning

Describe the number and use of work areas, restrooms, breakrooms, entrances, and any shared tenant spaces. Mention normal business hours, security procedures, and whether cleaning is needed during the workday or after closing.
