Do you do post renovation cleaning for apartments?

Post renovation apartment cleaning can address the agreed dust and surface cleaning needs after renovation work is complete and the area is suitable for service. Construction debris, hazardous materials and specialized residue removal should be assessed separately rather than assumed part of an ordinary cleaning visit.

Confirm that the renovation area is ready

Trades may still be creating dust, moving materials or installing fixtures after a room appears finished. Ask the property team to identify when the space will be available for cleaning. A final visit arranged too early can be followed by new soil from ongoing work. The provider needs a clear scope and access to the surfaces involved.

Distinguish surface soil from damage or hazards

Paint spots, adhesive, damaged finishes and unknown dust can require different treatment or another qualified provider. Do not ask a cleaner to improvise around suspected hazardous material. Share the work history and manufacturer information for new surfaces where available. Cleaning should preserve those materials rather than use aggressive removal simply to eliminate every visible mark.

Protect residents and shared routes

Renovation in one unit can affect common corridors and elevators used by other residents. Identify whether those areas are included and coordinate access appropriately. The apartment interior and shared spaces may need separate scopes. A clear handoff helps management understand which areas were completed before contractors, residents or prospective tenants return.

Respect new materials and ongoing work

New floors, glass and fittings may have care requirements or residue that does not belong to routine cleaning. Share the manufacturer information available and explain which trades are still active. A provider should not assume that an aggressive removal method is appropriate merely because a mark appeared during renovation.

Coordinate the shared route as well as the unit interior. Contractors can create new soil in corridors after the apartment itself has been cleaned. Identify who prepares these areas and whether another visit is needed after remaining work finishes. This distinguishes new residue from work that was missed during an earlier appointment.

Consider residents and shared spaces together

Apartment cleaning takes place in a community where residents continue to use entrances, corridors and shared facilities. Explain access rules and which belongings must remain untouched. A cleaning visit should not become an assumption that personal property can be discarded or occupied units can be entered without authorization. Management remains responsible for property rules and maintenance decisions outside the cleaning agreement. When a shared space changes or a new amenity opens, review the materials and duties before adding it to the program. This keeps service useful to the community while making the boundary between cleaning, management and repairs understandable to everyone involved.

A practical example

A newly renovated unit may be ready for surface cleaning while the shared hallway still contains contractor materials. The property should coordinate those responsibilities so the cleaning crew is not expected to remove construction waste outside its agreement.
